# Pre-registered test: TOI-2457 (TIC 117799904), 3.2911-day signal, TESS Sectors 109 and 110

The prediction was generated at 02:44 UTC on 2026-10-11 and first committed at 02:45:58 UTC
(commit 963e1a1 of the analysis code), before any Sector 109 or 110 data for this star were public.
This text was revised twice later the same day, still before any of those data were public, to
correct its wording and to specify the test completely (see "Revisions" at the end); the prediction,
the machine-readable file `toi2457_p3.2911.json` in this folder and the pass and fail criteria are
unchanged. The code that runs the test, and the environment it needs, are in the repository that
holds this file.

## The signal being tested

A transit-like signal at P = 3.2911037 d, about 480 ppm deep and 1.94 h long, around TIC 117799904
(TOI-2457, a K dwarf, Tmag 10.87), in addition to the known candidate TOI-2457.01
(14.1994 ± 0.0040 d on ExoFOP-TESS). It was found blind by this project's search in QLP light
curves (Sectors 32 and 98), and a separate blind search of the SPOC 2-minute light curves of
Sectors 32, 98 and 106 also recovers it. An ephemeris and depth fitted to the QLP data of Sectors 32
and 98 alone predicted 453 ± 69 ppm in Sector 106, where the SPOC light curve gives 447 ± 103 ppm
at the predicted times (roughly 3 to 4σ in one sector, depending on processing). Sector 106 is also
part of the prior below, so this supports the signal but does not confirm it.

The test asks whether the signal persists, at the frozen times and depth, in data taken after the
prediction. Passing it would be further evidence that the signal is real; it would not show that the
signal comes from a planet, nor that it comes from TOI-2457 rather than a blended neighbour.

## Test rules

Implemented in `scripts/prereg_test.py`, run as
`uv run python scripts/prereg_test.py docs/preregistration/toi2457_p3.2911.json 109,110`.
The equations and settings it uses are listed under "Test specification" below.

When to run it: as soon as SPOC 2-minute light curves of TIC 117799904 for both Sectors 109 and 110
are public on MAST. TIC 117799904 is on the official 2-minute target lists of both sectors. If
either sector's SPOC light curve is still not public on 31 January 2027, the test is run on that
date, using QLP for the missing sector. The test is run once, on the first versions retrieved.

Data and processing:

1. SPOC 2-minute PDCSAP light curves of TIC 117799904 for Sectors 109 and 110 (QLP only under the
   rule above).
2. Detrend exactly as the search does (`search.prepare`), masking the transits of TOI-2457.01 and
   of the candidate itself, and remove TOI-2457.01's transits from the fit.
3. Fit only the depth, with shape and ephemeris fixed to the frozen values.

Coverage: a predicted transit counts when at least half of its in-transit cadences exist after
processing. If fewer than 8 transits count, the result is **inconclusive**.

| Test | Pass when |
| --- | --- |
| T1 detection | depth / error ≥ 3, error = larger of white-noise and 200-phase empirical error |
| T2 consistency | the new depth agrees with 485 ± 65 ppm within 3σ |
| T3 cycle count | in the new data alone, k = 0 fits better than k = −1 and k = +1 and better than a flat line |

Verdict: **signal test passed** if T1, T2 and T3 all pass; **signal test not passed** if any fails
with at least 8 transits counted; **inconclusive** otherwise. The timing offset of a free-epoch fit
is reported but not judged. A pass is evidence that the signal persists; it does not validate a
planet or identify the host star. The script, which predates these names, prints CONFIRMED for
"signal test passed" and NOT CONFIRMED for "signal test not passed".

The result will be reported whatever it is. The rules will not change after Sector 109 or 110
data become public; any extra analysis will be labelled as post hoc.

## Test specification

This section describes what `scripts/prereg_test.py` and the modules it imports do, at the commit
that adds this text; it does not change the rules above.

Light curves. SPOC: PDCSAP_FLUX, cadences with QUALITY = 0 only, each sector divided by its median.
QLP (only under the fallback): SYS_RM_FLUX (or SAP_FLUX for older-format files), rejecting the
quality bits listed as BAD_BITS in `src/astrohunt/lightcurves.py`, each sector divided by its
median. Times are the light curves' BTJD (BJD_TDB − 2457000).

Detrending, per sector (`search.detrend_sector`):

1. Mean-bin to 10 minutes (bins start at the first cadence; empty bins are dropped).
2. Fit a wotan biweight trend with window 1.0 d, break_tolerance 0.5 d and edge_cutoff 0.25 d, so
   0.25 d is removed at each end of every stretch of data separated by a gap longer than 0.5 d.
   Cadences within ±0.75 durations of the transits of TOI-2457.01 (P = 14.193404 d,
   T0 = 2175.258623 BTJD, duration 0.106208 d) and of the candidate's search ephemeris
   (P = 3.2911057 d, T0 = 2176.238231 BTJD, duration 0.081218 d) are ignored when fitting the trend.
3. Divide by the trend. The per-point error σ is 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation of the
   sector's detrended flux, excluding the masked cadences. Points above 1 + 4σ are removed.

Cadences within ±0.75 TOI-2457.01 durations of its transits are then excluded from every fit.

Template. A batman transit model (LEO-Vetter's TransitModel) with Rp/R★ = 0.0189394,
a/R★ = 13.15508, impact parameter 0.1, quadratic limb darkening u1 = 0.409 and u2 = 0.2526; its
central depth is 435.7 ppm and its duration 0.080833 d. Its period and mid-time are set to the
frozen ephemeris (or to an alias in T3).

Depth. With m the model, s = 1 − m (zero outside transit), weights w = 1/σ² and detrended flux f,
the fitted depth is d = D₀ × Σ w (1 − f) s / Σ w s², where D₀ is the largest value of s among the
fitted cadences (close to the central depth), and the white-noise error is e_w = D₀ / √(Σ w s²).
There is no separate local baseline: the detrended level is taken as 1.

Empirical error. 200 shifts drawn uniformly between 0.15 and 0.85 of the period (numpy
`default_rng(0)`) are each added to the mid-time, and the depth is refitted on the same cadences at
each shifted ephemeris; e_emp is 1.4826 times the median absolute deviation of those depths. Shifts
with fewer than 3 in-transit cadences are skipped, and fewer than 10 usable shifts leave e_emp
undefined. The error used is e = max(e_w, e_emp), or e_w if e_emp is undefined.

T1: d / e ≥ 3. T2: |d − 484.76 ppm| / √(e² + (64.66 ppm)²) ≤ 3.

T3: for each of k = −1, 0 and +1, take the alias period and mid-time from the frozen alias table in
the JSON file and the template at its own fixed depth (not refitted), and compute
χ²(model) − χ²(flat) over the cadences within ±3 durations of that alias's transits, where
χ² = Σ ((f − m) / σ)² and the flat model is the weighted mean flux of those cadences. T3 passes if
the k = 0 value is negative and strictly lower than both the k = −1 and the k = +1 values.

Coverage. With δ the median spacing of the processed cadences (10 minutes), a predicted transit
counts when the number of processed cadences within ±0.5 durations of its mid-time is at least
0.5 × duration / δ, that is at least 6 ten-minute points. Transits that do not count still
contribute their cadences to the depth fit.

## Expected outcome

The Sector 106 dry run of the same script (data already in the prior, so not a test) counts 6
transits and measures 447 ± 103 ppm. If the new data provide comparable independent information,
two sectors would give an error near 75 ppm and a 480 ppm signal would reach S/N ≈ 6, so detection
would be expected with high probability. Reduced coverage, correlated noise, or a mismatch between
the true and the frozen ephemeris or shape would lower that probability; an independent simulation
built from Sector 106 residuals gave pass rates of about 93 per cent for a 450 ppm signal and
72 per cent for 360 ppm. A non-detection should therefore be judged against the precision and
coverage actually achieved, and "not passed" is not the same as "disproved".

## Known caveats

- The prior sectors produced the detection, so the prior depth may be biased high (selection bias).
  T2's 3σ window allows for that.
- A QLP search signal at 6.6106 d was dropped from the masks. An ad hoc check of the SPOC data with
  this project's template gave it only 190 ± 93 ppm once the 3.29-day transits are excluded, with
  inconsistent depths by sector (+425, +150, −142 ppm); an independent box measurement gave
  245 ± 108 ppm (+508, +184, +208 ppm). Either way it is weak, and masking it would only discard
  candidate transits.
- Release timing: SPOC 2-minute data reached MAST about 32 days after the end of Sectors 98 and 106,
  and QLP about 24 days after Sector 98; these are past lags, not commitments. Sector 109 ends on
  2026-10-31.

   specification" section, which describes the existing code; labels the UTC column as a timescale

   near an orbit start; qualifies the power estimate; and labels the 6.6106 d numbers as an ad hoc
   check. The prediction, the JSON file and the pass and fail criteria did not change.
